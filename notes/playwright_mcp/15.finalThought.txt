15 Final thoughts
------------------ 
AI agents are powerful. They can dramatically speed up test authoring and maintenance. 
But they are not a substitute for engineering fundamentals.

Here is what the practice actually looks like:
Without fundamentals ==>
AI generates plausible-looking tests. You accept them. Coverage grows but quality drops silently. 
Your suite starts catching fewer real bugs. You do not notice until something ships broken.

With fundamentals ==>
AI generates the first draft. You review it critically. You reject weak locators, weak assertions, 
and silent skips. Coverage grows AND quality holds. AI becomes leverage.

The AI does not replace the engineer. The AI amplifies whatever the engineer already is. 
If the engineer is strong at fundamentals, the AI makes them stronger. 
If the engineer is weak at fundamentals, the AI ships weakness faster.

Three habits worth building : 
-----------------------------
- Read every diff. Every line of AI-generated code deserves the same review as human-written code.
- Distrust green builds from Healers. A test that suddenly passes after a Healer session is a suspect until proven otherwise.
- Codify your team rules once. Put them in AGENTS.md and the agent files. The AI will follow them. What you skip codifying, the AI will do its own way.

The role of a test engineer is not changing to "prompt writer." 
The role is changing to reviewer, guardrail, and architect. 
Those skills come from fundamentals, not from AI.

Use AI.
Review harder than you write.
